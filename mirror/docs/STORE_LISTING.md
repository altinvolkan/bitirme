Kurallar: başlık ≤ 30, kısa açıklama ≤ 80, uzun açıklama ≤ 4000 karakter. Apple adı yalnızca düz yazıyla ve açıklayıcı biçimde geçer; Apple/AirPlay logoları görsellerde kullanılmaz. Her dil Play Console'da "Mağaza girişi > Çeviriler" altına girilir.

## en

**Başlık (25):** iMirror: Screen Mirroring

**Kısa açıklama (78):** Mirror your Android screen and sound to Apple TV over Wi-Fi, with low latency.

**Uzun açıklama:**

Mirror your Android screen to Apple TV over your home Wi-Fi.

• Direct mirroring: no extra app is needed on the Apple TV.
• Also works with Android TV through the free iMirror TV receiver app.
• Low latency, with a latency buffer you can adjust.
• System audio is sent to the TV too.
• Rotate your phone and the picture adapts automatically.
• Pairing is remembered, so the code is entered only once.

Free to use with ads and a small watermark. iMirror Premium removes ads and the watermark, and unlocks 60 fps.

Good to know: apps with DRM protection (such as Netflix or Spotify) cannot be mirrored. For the best results use a 5 GHz Wi-Fi network, stay close to the router and connect the Apple TV with an Ethernet cable.

iMirror is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple Inc. Apple TV and AirPlay are trademarks of Apple Inc.
